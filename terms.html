Free use

The App can be used without paying. Free use includes a set number of new cards each day, unlimited reviews of cards you have already met, and part of the reading library. These limits are shown in the App and may change in future versions.

Svenska Pro

Svenska Pro removes the daily limit on new cards and opens the full reading library, all grammar drills, the placement test and your personal path. It is offered as:
